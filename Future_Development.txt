## **1. Body detection system**

    **Camera layer** – grabs frames from webcam.
    **Pose model** – finds keypoints: body joints, hands, fingers, head, spine.
    **Gesture layer** – interprets keypoints into “actions”
    (✌️ = peace, ✋ = stop, full-body pose = pause media, finger gesture = command).
    **Tracking layer** – maintains smooth motion tracking even when moving fast.
    **JARVIS integration** – converts gestures into commands (volume up, open apps, scroll).
    **framework** – MediaPipe Holistic, OpenPose, MoveNet.


## **2. Emotion detection system**

    **Camera/Mic layer** – captures face or audio input.
    **Emotion model** – classifies emotions (happy, sad, angry, fear, surprise, neutral).
    **Analysis layer** – cleans and stabilizes emotion predictions.
    **JARVIS integration** – adjusts responses or UI based on mood.
    **framework** – FER, DeepFace, MediaPipe FaceMesh.


## **3. Weather detection system**

    **Location layer** – detects user location via GPS/IP.
    **API layer** – fetches real-time weather + forecast.
    **Analysis layer** – interprets rain alerts, storms, UV index.
    **JARVIS integration** – displays or speaks conditions.
    **framework** – OpenWeather API.


## **4. QR code scanner**

    **Camera layer** – scans frames for QR markers.
    **Decode engine** – extracts encoded data.
    **Action layer** – opens links or processes commands.
    **JARVIS integration** – executes QR-triggered tasks.
    **framework** – Pyzbar, OpenCV QRDetector.


## **5. JARVIS playing tic-tac-toe**

    **Game engine** – grid logic + winner detection.
    **AI layer** – minimax algorithm for unbeatable AI.
    **UI layer** – displays board visually.
    **JARVIS integration** – voice-based gameplay.
    **framework** – Minimax, Tkinter/Pygame.


## **6. Voice-based emotion detection**

    **Audio processing layer** – extracts MFCC, tone, pitch, rhythm.
    **Emotion model** – detects mood from speech.
    **Response layer** – modifies JARVIS personality dynamically.
    **JARVIS integration** – reacts emotionally to your voice tone.
    **framework** – librosa, Audio Emotion Models.


## **7. Object detection**

    **Camera layer** – captures frame stream.
    **Detection model** – finds phones, people, laptops, books, etc.
    **Tracking layer** – follows moving objects.
    **JARVIS integration** – warns or acts on detected items.
    **framework** – YOLOv8, SSD-MobileNet.


## **8. Face recognition**

    **Camera layer** – captures face frames.
    **Embedding model** – converts faces into 128D vectors.
    **Compare layer** – matches with known users.
    **JARVIS integration** – access control + personalized greetings.
    **framework** – FaceNet, DeepFace.


## **9. Offline maps**

    **Storage layer** – saves map tiles on device.
    **Routing engine** – calculates directions offline.
    **UI layer** – map viewer + zoom + navigation.
    **JARVIS integration** – voice navigation commands.
    **framework** – OpenStreetMap, Mapbox Offline.


## **10. Data hiding in plain sight (Steganography)**

    **Encode engine** – hides data inside images (LSB).
    **Decode engine** – extracts hidden data.
    **Security layer** – optional encryption before hiding.
    **JARVIS integration** – secret data vault in images.
    **framework** – LSB Steganography, OpenCV.


## **11. Voice translator**

    **Speech-to-text layer** – converts speech to text.
    **Translation model** – converts language in real-time.
    **Text-to-speech layer** – speaks translated output.
    **JARVIS integration** – multilingual conversation assistant.
    **framework** – Google Translate API, Whisper.


## **12. Text detection system**

    **Camera layer** – reads documents or screens.
    **OCR engine** – extracts text from images.
    **Processing layer** – cleans, straightens, and enhances text.
    **JARVIS integration** – reads aloud, saves notes, converts to PDF.
    **framework** – Tesseract OCR, EasyOCR.
