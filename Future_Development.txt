body detection system
    Camera layer – grabs frames from webcam.

    Pose model – finds keypoints: body joints + hands + fingers (like your image).

    Gesture layer – interprets those keypoints into “actions”
        (✌️ = peace, ✋ = stop, full-body pose = pause media, etc.).

    JARVIS integration – when a gesture is recognized, trigger JARVIS commands.

    framework - MediaPipe Holistic, OpenPose, MoveNet.
